# Combined Furnace

A simple Minecraft mod that adds a new furnace which smelts **all** items at Blast Furnace / Smoker speed.

## Features

- Works exactly like the vanilla Furnace
- Smelts every item at the speed of a Blast Furnace or Smoker
- Designed to stay balanced and fit naturally into Minecraft’s progression

## Crafting

To craft the Combined Furnace you need:

- 1× Furnace
- 1× Smoker
- 1× Blast Furnace
- Nether Bricks
- Gold Blocks

This means you have to reach the Nether before you can obtain it. The recipe gives the furnace a small progression requirement without making it unnecessarily difficult to obtain.

Once crafted, it simply provides the convenience of having **one** furnace that can smelt everything at Blast Furnace / Smoker speed.
